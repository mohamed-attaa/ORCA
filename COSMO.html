About Cosmo

Where Sophistication
Meets The Night

Cosmo Lounge & Club is the embodiment of refined nightlife. With a capacity capped at 200 guests, we offer an intimate, sophisticated atmosphere where every detail is designed for those who appreciate the finer things.

From the curated music selection to the handcrafted cocktails, every element at Cosmo is chosen to create an unforgettable experience. Our space balances modern elegance with warm hospitality, making it the perfect destination for both relaxed evenings and celebratory nights.

Open from 9 PM to 4 AM, Cosmo offers the ideal setting for those who seek a night that's both vibrant and refined.
